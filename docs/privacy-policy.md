# Voice Vault Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Voice Vault is an Android app that records a short voice note, transcribes it on your
device, and saves it as a Markdown file in a folder you choose. This policy explains
what the app does with your data. The short version: it collects nothing.

## Data we collect

None. Voice Vault has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.
The developer receives no information about you or how you use the app.

## Microphone and audio

The app uses the microphone only while a capture you started is recording. The
recording is kept in the app's private storage until it has been transcribed, then
deleted. Transcription runs entirely on your device. Audio and transcribed text are
never sent anywhere.

## Your notes

Notes are written as plain Markdown files into a folder on your device that you pick.
You control that folder, including whether any other app (such as a sync service)
reads it. Voice Vault only has access to the folder you selected.

## Network use

The app uses the internet once: on first launch it downloads a speech-recognition
model (about 57 MB) so that transcription can run offline. This request downloads a
file and sends none of your data. After the download, the app works fully offline.

## Permissions

- Microphone: to record voice notes.
- Notifications: to show recording status and report a note that could not be saved.
- Internet: only for the one-time model download.

## Data deletion

All data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app removes the model and any
recordings still waiting to be transcribed. Notes already saved to your folder are
yours and stay where they are.

## Children

Voice Vault is not directed at children under 13.
